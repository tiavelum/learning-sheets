Tank at work: changing jobs

Vested benefits

When you change workplace, the water in your compartment moves with you into the next tank, or waits in a sealed holding tank at the bank until there is one.

Tank at work: at retirement, tap or carry away

Pension-fund pension

The tap on your compartment: from retirement it lets the same flow into your garden every month for as long as you live.

or
Lump sum

Instead of only using the tap, you can carry part of your compartment's water away at once, or all of it if your tank's rules allow, and must make it last yourself.
