Page 1 of 2 · The picture

This sheet pictures logins as getting into houses: your own house at home, buildings in town, and emergency exits for when you are locked out. It is a picture, not the real thing; page two gives the facts behind each numbered item.

Your house and the town

At homeIn townIf lost
1

Passcode, face or fingerprint

The keypad on your front door: only you can open it, and you carry no key.

2

Phone or computer

Your house, drawn as the laptop and phone on the desk: the keypad (1) opens it; inside stands your key cabinet (3).

3

Password manager

The key cabinet in your house, with a key for every building in town except the bank, whose key you remember.

4

Password manager account

A bank in town that keeps a locked spare of your cabinet, so a new house can get it back.

In town · how you get in

You hand the key over.

5

Password

You hand your key over at each visit; whoever watches, fakes the door, or steals and unscrambles the building’s copy gets in.

6

Two-step login (2FA)

After you hand over the key, the door asks for a second proof: a fresh number from a code card you carry, or a yes you give the doorman.

vs

You keep the key.

7

Passkey

You keep the key: the door only checks that it fits, holds nothing worth stealing, and fake doors won’t accept it.

In town · other ways in

8

Email login link

The building drops a one-time key in your letterbox; whoever empties your letterbox walks in.

9

Sign in with another account

A pass from one big building that many others accept; whoever holds that pass gets into all of them.

If lost · the emergency exits

10

Recovery email or phone

The building posts a reset letter to the address it has on file for you.

11

Recovery phrase or key

A master spare on paper, kept outside the cabinet, that reopens a building for you when every other key is lost.

12

Recovery contact

A trusted neighbour who helps you back in with a code or by confirming it’s you, but can’t enter.

13

Identity check

The front desk asks for proof that you are the owner, then decides, sometimes after days, whether to let you in.

14

2FA recovery codes

A strip of spare numbers, each good once, for the second proof when your code card is lost. Adds to 6.
