TLS (HTTPS) Once it has checked the plaque (4), an armoured van carries your errands across the street; passers-by see the van but can't look inside or swap its load.

SSH From home, the building's caretaker gives orders through a sealed speaking tube into one machine room; nobody on the street can listen in.

VPN A private tunnel under the street brings you up into the company basement; traditionally, whoever comes through it counts as being inside.

Certificate The plaque at the entrance, stamped by a trusted registry, shows this really is the building at the address you headed for, not an imitation.

MFA (two-factor) At reception you give your password plus a fresh code from your phone, so a stolen password alone gets nobody in.

Passkey Instead of a password, a key kept on your own devices opens only this building's door, once your device has recognised you; the building never sees your fingerprint.

Zero Trust Each inside door checks who you are and what you may do before it opens, so being in the building, even through the tunnel, opens nothing by itself.

Numbers 7 and 8 happen inside too; they are shown below, side by side, because they are easy to mix up.
