Remember
  • The Three Ways (Gene Kim): Flow 10, Feedback 13, Continual learning 16.
  • CALMS: Culture, Automation, Lean, Measurement, Sharing.
  • Not a tool or one team: builders and operators share the work.
  • Fast and safe tend to go together: small, automated changes.
  • Agile builds in short steps; DevOps extends that to running it.
  • Security belongs in the routine, not at the end (DevSecOps).
  • Adopt one practice at a time: start where delivery hurts most.
Ask your team
  • How long does a one-line change take to reach users?
  • When something breaks, who notices first: we or the users?
  • After an incident, do we look for causes or for culprits?

Where it comes from: in 2009 John Allspaw and Paul Hammond showed Flickr doing "10+ deploys per day" through Dev and Ops cooperation; Patrick Debois then started DevOpsDays in Ghent, which named the movement.
