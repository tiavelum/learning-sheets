The scene, continued

These items are not drawn on page 1. Each block sits in the place where its items do their job.

Head office· two ways to write plan sheets for many stores

Helm chart

A ready-made plan template with blanks, such as store size, filled in for each store; chains share such templates with each other.

Kustomize

One finished base plan plus a few sticky notes per store that change single lines; there are no blanks to fill.

Store· how the plan reaches the store: the one contrast to remember

From outside: push
Push deployment

A courier with keys to every store pins up the new sheets and drives off; nobody comes back to check the board.

From inside: pull
Argo CD

A shelf keeper with a big status screen: it shows where the board differs from the binder and fixes it when someone presses Sync, or by itself if told to. It can also tend other stores, but then holds their keys.

Flux

A team of specialists living in each store: one fetches the plan, others pin up sheets or fill in templates, one sends messages. Each round, they fix what differs.

Progressive delivery works alongside 13 and 14

A trial shelf: a new product first fills one shelf end. If sales and complaints look good it takes over the aisle; if not, it comes off again.

Store· keeping the safe's code out of plain sight

Sealed Secrets / SOPS

Each store's safe has a code. It travels in the binder, sealed in an envelope that the store's own key opens.

External Secrets Operator

The binder only names a box at the bank; a clerk in the store fetches the code from there and asks again at set times, so the store's copy follows the bank's.

Building· the premises the store stands on: walls, power, cold rooms

Terraform / OpenTofu

A building contractor: compares the drawings with a logbook of what was built, shows what will change, builds when you say go, then leaves.

Crossplane

A caretaker living in the store who keeps the building matching its drawings all day, the way the staff keep the shelves.
