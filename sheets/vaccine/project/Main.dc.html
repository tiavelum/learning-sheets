How a vaccine trains your body

Page 1 of 2: the picture

The town is your body, its houses are your cells, neighbouring towns are other people and the burglar gang is a germ. A vaccine is the town's training drill; each number marks one part of it, and page two gives the facts.

Community: across many towns
Antigen
The gang's face on the wanted poster at the gate: the feature, or a few features, the town learns to recognise.
Adjuvant
An alarm bell rung beside some posters, so the patrol officers take a quiet poster seriously.
Dendritic cell
A patrol officer who picks up the face at the gate, carries it to the station and holds it up there for the other officers.
Lymph node
The station where the carried face is shown to passing officers, and where those who recognise it gather and multiply.
Helper T cell
The sergeant who recognises the face and gives the workshop officers and demolition officers the go-ahead.
B cell
An officer who also recognises the face and, once given the go-ahead, opens a workshop making tags that fit that one face.
Antibody
Once ready, a tag that clamps onto the gang's face in the street, so the gang cannot get into houses and street sweepers can round it up.
Killer T cell
Once ready, a demolition officer who spots houses showing the face in a window, a sign the gang may be inside, and brings them down, gang and all.
Herd immunity
When enough neighbouring towns know the face, the gang struggles to find a next town, so a town that cannot hold a drill is often spared too.

Show

vaccination day: four common ways a vaccine brings the face
Live-attenuated vaccine
A tamed gang member who slips into a few houses and multiplies there a while, usually too weak to harm, so the town meets one in person.
Inactivated vaccine
A life-size dummy of a gang member: the whole figure, but it cannot move or break in.
Subunit vaccine
Just one piece of the gang, such as its jacket or a blunted crowbar, so the town learns that piece alone.
mRNA vaccine
A print order that has houses near the gate print the face poster for a while, then is shredded without entering the house's records.

Side effects
Bustle and roadblocks near the gate and a tired town for a few days: usually the drill at work, not the gang.

Remember

months and years: first time slow, next time fast
Memory cells
Veteran officers with the face on file, plus a few workshops kept open, so the town turns out within days if the gang comes.
Booster
A repeat drill, with the same or an updated face, that refills a fading file and sharpens the tags.
